Frequently Asked Questions

  • Why is the pricing only ₹9999? +
    We keep our prices affordable because we believe in volume over margins. By streamlining our processes and using modern automation tools, we can deliver premium quality websites at prices that don't burden small businesses. Our goal is to help every business go digital without breaking the bank.
  • What pages are included in the package? +
    Your package includes up to 5 main pages such as Home, About Us, Services/Products, Testimonials, and Contact. These pages are fully customizable based on your business needs and are designed to convert visitors into customers.
  • Is the website mobile responsive? +
    Yes, absolutely! Every website we build is 100% mobile responsive. Your site will look perfect on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. Mobile optimization is critical in today's digital landscape and we never compromise on this.
  • Will SEO be included? +
    Yes! SEO is built into every website. We optimize your site structure, implement proper heading hierarchy, optimize images, ensure fast loading speeds, and configure Google Search Console. Your website will be ready to rank on Google from day one.
  • How long does development take? +
    Typically, we deliver websites within 10-15 business days. This includes design, development, SEO optimization, and testing. We can offer expedited delivery for an additional fee if you need your website sooner.
  • Can I upgrade or add more pages later? +
    Absolutely! You can upgrade at any time. Additional pages cost ₹2000 per page. We also offer hourly maintenance support if you need regular updates or new features added to your website.
  • Do you provide hosting? +
    We provide hosting guidance and can recommend reliable hosting providers. We can also integrate your site with popular hosting platforms. Domain registration is your responsibility, but we guide you through the entire process.
  • Will WhatsApp integration be included? +
    Yes! We include WhatsApp integration in every package. Your customers can easily chat with you directly from your website, making it convenient for them and increasing conversion rates for your business.
